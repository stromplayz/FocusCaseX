What it is

Everything in FocusCaseX starts from three ideas.

Pages are markdown

Every clinical page is a plain markdown file — readable, printable, yours. No opaque database, no lock-in.

Capsules are building blocks

Paste a two-line block and a live board renders — appointments, vitals, recalls. Drop them into any page.

Offline-first, always

Runs with no internet, on your device. Share the workspace over LAN so your staff sees the same clinic.
