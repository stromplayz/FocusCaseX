Install guide

How to install

Installing takes one tap — this is the whole process.

Everything installs from inside FocusCaseX. This page just shows you around.

Install any add-on

  1. Open FocusCaseX → Settings → Marketplace.
  2. Search for what you need — try the name, or browse by Extensions, Capsules, Themes or Templates.
  3. Tap Install. It appears in your workspace right away.
Free · works offline after install · remove any time.

What each kind does

Remove any time

Settings → Marketplace → Installed → tap Remove. Installing or removing never touches your patient records.

Questions

Is everything here really free?
Yes. Every add-on installs at no cost, and there is nothing to buy anywhere in FocusCaseX.
Do I need this website to install anything?
No — the full catalog is inside the app under Marketplace. This site is for browsing ahead of time and sharing links with your team.
Will it work offline, like the rest of FocusCaseX?
Yes. Once installed, add-ons work on your device with no internet, same as your records.
Who makes these — can I trust them?
Most are built and reviewed by the CaseX team; the shield badge means reviewed. Every item shows its author, version and last-update date.
What's the difference between extensions, capsules and templates?
Extensions add features, capsules are ready-made page sections, themes change the look, and templates are pre-written clinical notes and forms.
What if an add-on doesn't suit my clinic?
Remove it in one tap under Settings → Marketplace. Installing or removing never touches your patient records.
