WorkBenches

One install. Whole EMR.

A WorkBench is one installable bundle that contains a complete, pre-configured EMR setup — extensions, capsules, a theme, templates, snippets, starter pages and settings, put together for one way of working. Install a WorkBench and the whole workspace is set up in a single step, instead of assembling add-ons one by one.

4 WorkBenches at launch · every piece in a bundle is listed before you confirm · free

What's inside

Every kind the marketplace carries, plus the pieces only a bundle can bring.

Extensions

Small tools that add features — exam composers, visit trackers, patient cloning.

Capsules

Ready-made sections — boards, registers, histories — that render live in your pages.

Theme

One look for the whole workspace, applied the moment the bundle lands.

Templates

Pre-written clinical notes and forms, filed into your page tree.

Snippets

Small CSS touches that adjust heading sizes, spacing and the reading view.

Starter pages

A dashboard, day book and clinic pages, ready to edit from first open.

Settings

Workspace preferences tuned to the way the clinic works — sidebars, defaults, views.

The first four

Each installs every piece it lists — confirmed once, in the app.

Tap Install and your browser hands the bundle to the FocusCaseX app on this device — the app lists everything the WorkBench adds and asks you to confirm. No app on this device? Type the code under Settings → Marketplace → Install by code, or see the install guide.

One confirm before anything lands The app lists every extension, capsule, template and page the WorkBench adds. You confirm once — the whole bundle installs.
Remove piece by piece Anything a WorkBench installs can be removed later under Settings — the bundle adds tools and pages, it never moves your patient records.

Build your own

Your setup, saved as one bundle.

Any clinic can turn its current workspace into a WorkBench. In the app, open Settings → Marketplace → Save as WorkBench — FocusCaseX packs your extensions, capsules, theme, templates, snippets, pages and settings into one shareable bundle. Hand it to a second branch or a colleague and they install the whole setup in one step. The WorkBench Builder on the MCP & Automation page can also draft a starter bundle from a plain description of how your clinic works.
